Watch - read me

INSTALL
1. Drag Watch onto Applications.
2. Eject this disk image and open Watch from Applications.
3. Click Start Watching in the welcome window. Watch lives in the menu bar.

Watch isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS will probably block the first launch.
Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click
Open Anyway next to Watch. You'll need to do this again after each update.
Only download Watch from github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases.

USING IT
Click the play icon in the menu bar, or press Option-Command-Y.
Type to search YouTube, or paste a link and press Return.
Click a result to play it. The arrow button next to it downloads it.
Music or a podcast playing in another app shows up at the top of the list.
Click it to open the artwork.
Searching and most public videos work without signing in.

WHAT'S INSIDE
yt-dlp, ffmpeg, ffprobe and Deno are inside the app, so there's nothing else to
install. If something doesn't work, try Preferences > Setup Guide > Check Tools
Again. If a tool is missing, download Watch again.
Needs macOS 13 or newer. Intel Macs haven't been tested on real hardware.

SIGN IN TO YOUTUBE (OPTIONAL)
You only need this for History, Playlists, and videos that ask you to sign in.
Open Preferences > YouTube Account > Sign In, pick Chrome or Safari, sign in to
YouTube in that browser, then come back and click Connect.
- Chrome may ask to use its "Chrome Safe Storage" Keychain item.
- Safari may need Full Disk Access for Watch, and a quit and reopen.
Your Google password goes into the browser, never into Watch. Sign Out
disconnects Watch and leaves the browser signed in. YouTube changes things
now and then, so this can stop working.

UPDATING
Download the new version, quit Watch, drag the new app onto Applications and
choose Replace. Your links, favourites and settings are kept. The bundled
tools update along with the app.

LICENSES
Notices and source for the bundled tools are in Preferences > Setup Guide >
Open Third-Party Notices.
