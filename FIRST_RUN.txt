Watch 3.18 — start here

INSTALL
1. Drag Watch.app onto Applications.
2. Eject this disk image, then open Watch from Applications.
3. Click Start Watching in the welcome guide. Watch lives in your menu bar.

This personal preview is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch,
open System Settings > Privacy & Security and look for Open Anyway after trying
to open Watch. Approve only the copy you received from your trusted sender.
If that option is unavailable, ask your sender for a signed/notarized installer.
No Terminal commands or disabling system security are required by Watch.

WATCH
Click the play icon in the menu bar, or press Option-Command-Y.
Type to search YouTube. Paste a video link and press Return to play it.
Click a result to play; click its down-arrow button to download.
Recent Links is fully listed: scroll to see older videos.
Public search and most public videos do not require signing in.

EVERYTHING IS INCLUDED
Watch includes yt-dlp, ffmpeg, ffprobe and Deno. No Homebrew, Python, Terminal,
or separate helper downloads are needed. Apple Silicon and Intel are included.
Requires macOS 13 or newer. Intel and macOS 13 hardware acceptance is still pending.
Use Preferences > Setup Guide > Check Tools Again if something isn't working.
If a tool is missing or won't start, replace Watch with a fresh installer copy.

CONNECT YOUTUBE (OPTIONAL)
Open Preferences > YouTube Account > Sign In.
Pick Chrome or Safari, sign in to YouTube in that browser, and return to Watch.
Click Connect and follow the permission instructions:
- Chrome can ask for access to its Chrome Safe Storage Keychain item.
- Safari can require Full Disk Access for Watch and a quit/reopen.
Grant these only if you want Watch to read your browser's YouTube session.
Your Google password is entered in the browser, not in Watch.
History, Playlists and restricted videos depend on YouTube and may be unavailable
if YouTube changes its service or denies access. Sign Out disconnects Watch;
your browser stays signed in. No one else's account or cookies are in this installer.

UPDATES
Ask your sender for a newer Watch installer. Quit Watch, drag the new app into
Applications, and choose Replace. Your library and preferences stay in place.
Media tools update with the app; Watch does not alter the signed helper files.

The included third-party notices and source archives are accessible through
Setup Guide > Open Third-Party Notices (inside Watch.app/Contents/Resources).
