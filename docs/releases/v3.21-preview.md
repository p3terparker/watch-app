# Watch 3.21 preview — Up Next

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer 3.21 build passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18; the 3.19 and 3.20 previews stay available.

## New: one queue for everything

- **Up Next drops down under the music window**, like Music's MiniPlayer. Click **≡** next to "…" on the music window, press **⌥⌘U**, or choose **Up Next** from its menu. It shows **Now Playing**, **Playing Next** (what you added), the rest of the list, and **Then: Continue in Music/Spotify**. Played items are under **History**.
- **Play Next / Play Later** on any video or song in Watch: right-click a row in Recent Links, Favourites, search results, History or a playlist, or click the new **+** beside a row (⌥-click = Play Next). In the field, **⌥Return** adds a pasted link or file instead of playing it. Drop several files or links on the menu-bar icon (or hold ⌥ while dropping one) to add them. Other apps can use `glasskit-watch://queue?url=…`.
- **Mix sources:** YouTube and other sites, MP3 and other audio files, direct audio links and **Spotify** songs and episodes (paste or drag a Spotify link) all go in the same list. Files and audio links play in the music window itself; videos open in a video window beside it when their turn comes; Spotify songs play in the Spotify app.
- **It slots into what's already playing.** If Music or Spotify is playing when you add something, Watch waits for the current song to end, pauses that app and plays your items. When Up Next runs out, the app carries on with its own Up Next. (Apple doesn't let other apps read Music's own queue, so Watch can't list what Music will play afterwards.) macOS asks once, the first time, for Watch to control that app.
- **Edit it:** drag rows to reorder, **⌫** removes, double-click or Return plays a row, **Clear** removes what you added. Drop files, folders or links anywhere in the list. Right-click a row for Play Now, Play Next, Remove, Show in Finder / Open in Browser / Open in Spotify, Copy Link and Favourites.
- **The music window plays your queued songs**, with the same one-row controls, timeline and volume. Double-click opens the item where it lives: a file in Finder, a video's page at the current time, a song in Spotify. Shuffle and repeat stay with Music and Spotify.
- If you pick something else in Music or Spotify yourself, Watch lets go and the list shows **Resume**. Pressing next with a media key while the app is in the background moves on through Up Next instead.

## Install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

Built and tested automatically on GitHub's Mac: the whole app compiles. The unit checks cover the queue's ordering and editing, the song-boundary rules and Spotify links. The window checks use a stand-in Music app and test files made by the bundled FFmpeg, so no real Music, Spotify or account is used. Hand-offs with the real Music and Spotify apps, and Spotify's own behaviour at the end of a song (a split second of its next song may be heard before Watch pauses it), need trying by hand. That's what this preview is for.
