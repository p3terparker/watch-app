# Watch 3.25 preview — a calmer music window

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer 3.25 build passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18; the 3.19–3.24 previews stay available.

## New

- **The music window's controls only appear when you want them.** Hovering over the artwork shows the title and the "…" button as before, but the playback controls now come up only when the pointer rests on the title or just above it, so they are there as you reach for them. They ease in and out, and stay a moment after the pointer leaves, so they never snap away.
- **Faster clicks in the music window.** A click plays or pauses after about a third of a second instead of half a second. A double-click that opens the song in its app (or skips 10 seconds at the edges) now has to be quick; a slower second click is simply another click.
- Video windows are unchanged.

## Install

1. Download **Watch-3.25.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

Built and tested automatically on GitHub's Mac: the whole app compiles. The unit checks cover the area that reveals the controls (the title band plus a short reach above it, larger once they are up, and small windows) and the click timing (single, quick double, a late second click counted as a new click, stacking at the edges). The window checks drive the real music window with a stand-in Music app: hovering the artwork shows the title but not the controls, resting just above the title reveals them after a short dwell, a quick sweep doesn't, they go after a delay and not at once, a single click plays or pauses after the shorter wait, a quick double-click opens the app, a slow second click doesn't, and edge double-clicks still skip and stack. How the reveal and the click timing actually feel (the dwell, the reach, the easing) needs trying by hand with a mouse and a trackpad. That's what this preview is for.
