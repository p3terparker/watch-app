# Watch 3.23 preview — Up Next everywhere, your Music library

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer 3.23 build passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18; the 3.19–3.22 previews stay available.

## New

- **Up Next in every video window.** Press **⌥⌘U** (or choose **Show Up Next List** from the "…" menu) and the window's own list drops down under it, just like the music window's: the playlist, search results, folder or YouTube's next videos it's playing through. Drag to reorder, **⌫** removes, double-click plays, and drop videos, files or links on it to play them next in that window.
- **Your Music library in the search.** Search results now have a third tab, **Music**, which searches the songs in your Music library. Click one to play it in Music, or **+** to add it to Up Next with your videos, files, podcasts and Spotify songs. Watch only looks while Music is open and you've allowed it — the tab offers **Open Music** or **Allow…** when needed (macOS asks once).
- **Music and Spotify carry on.** When Up Next began with a Music or Spotify song, that app continues with its own Up Next after the list ends.

## Install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

Built and tested automatically on GitHub's Mac: the whole app compiles. The unit checks cover the library search script (including awkward characters in a search), reading Music's answer and the song IDs Watch accepts. The window checks use stand-in answers instead of Music: the Music tab and its buttons, a song playing through Up Next, and a video window's own list with a dropped file. Searching and playing your real library, and the one-time permission prompt, need trying by hand. That's what this preview is for.
