# Watch 3.20 preview — music window controls

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer 3.20 build passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18; the 3.19 preview stays available as it was.

## New in the music window

- **One row of controls under the title:** previous / play-pause / next on the left, then the timeline, then **shuffle** and **repeat**, then the volume — all on one line. In a small window the elapsed time gives way first, then shuffle/repeat, then the volume slider (the speaker stays).
- **Shuffle and repeat** change **Music's or Spotify's own** settings (not Watch's). They light up when on. In Music, repeat goes off → all → this song; Spotify only has on/off. macOS asks once, the first time you click one.
- **Easier to reach:** the controls appear as soon as the pointer is anywhere over the window and stay while it's there. They fade about a second after it leaves, so overshooting the edge doesn't make them vanish.
- **Clicks:**
  - Click anywhere: play / pause. A click waits for a moment, so it doesn't also fire when you double-click.
  - Double-click: open the music in its app (Music shows the song; Spotify, Podcasts and other apps come forward).
  - Double-click near the far left or right edge: back / forward 10 s. Keep clicking to go further.
- **Full screen** is now on **F**, **⌃⌘F** or the "…" menu (double-click opens the app instead).

Video windows are unchanged.

## Install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

Built and tested automatically: the whole app compiles, the unit checks (including the new control row, click zones and shuffle/repeat scripts) and the isolated window checks pass on GitHub's Mac with a fixture music app. How the reveal and click timing feel, and shuffle/repeat against the real Music and Spotify, need trying by hand — that's what this preview is for.
