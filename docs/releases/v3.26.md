A smaller update on 3.25: a few things for the music window, a volume control that works at any size, and an optional song name in the menu bar.

## What's new

### Music

- **★ for Apple Music.** A star next to shuffle and repeat favourites the song that's playing in Music itself. It happens in the background (Music doesn't come forward) and the star shows Music's real state. It's separate from Watch's own Favourites. Spotify has no star: it doesn't let other apps like a song.
- **Full screen you can resize.** Click and drag in the full-screen artwork to make the picture bigger or smaller, from a quarter of the usual size up to filling the screen. As it gets close to the full height, the title and controls slide down and fade away, and they're gone when it fills the screen from top to bottom. Make it smaller and they come back. Watch remembers the size.
- **The song in your menu bar (optional).** Turn on **Preferences ▸ Show the playing song and artist in the menu bar** for one small line, "Song – Artist", next to Watch's icon. Long names scroll like the old iTunes display; short ones stay still. It's dimmed and still while paused, and it disappears when nothing is playing. While it's on, Watch keeps an eye on what's playing in the background.
- **Up Next is on hold.** The little list button is gone from the music window while the queue is being reworked. Up Next is still in the right-click menu and the "…" menu (**⌥⌘U**).

### Volume

- **A slider that pops up.** The volume slider stays out of the way until you rest the pointer on the speaker, then a small vertical slider pops up above it. It works at any window size, big or small, in both the music window and video windows.
- **Click and drag the speaker.** Click it to mute or unmute. Press and drag up or down on it to change the volume from wherever it is now, the way Apple Music does. You don't have to aim at the slider, and the volume doesn't jump.
