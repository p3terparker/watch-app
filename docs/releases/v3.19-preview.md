# Watch 3.19 preview — Now Playing artwork

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer preview passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18.

## New

- **Now Playing:** the music playing in another app (Music, Spotify, Podcasts…) appears at the top of the panel with its artwork, title and artist. Click it to open the artwork in a floating window that follows the music. Right-click for Copy, Save, Open in Preview, Search YouTube and Show App. Turn it off in Preferences ▸ "Show what's playing in other apps".
- **Playback controls:** in the artwork window, rest the pointer on the title at the bottom and quiet controls slide in: a thin timeline you can drag, previous / play-pause / next, and for Music and Spotify a small volume slider for that app (macOS asks once, the first time you drag it). Watch only acts on the other app when you click one of these.
- **Full screen artwork:** double-click the top or bottom middle of the artwork (or press F, or use the "…" menu) to fill the screen. Choose a background of the artwork's own colours or black under "…" ▸ Full-Screen Background. The playback controls work there too. Esc leaves full screen.
- **Video player volume:** hover the speaker icon and a volume slider slides out; ↑/↓ change it too, and new windows start at your last volume. Click the icon to mute, as before.

## Fixed in build 51

- **YouTube links play in Watch's own player again,** not on YouTube's web page (signed out, with ads). When YouTube asks Watch to prove it isn't a bot, Watch now retries with your YouTube sign-in (Preferences ▸ YouTube Account). If a video still can't play, the window says why, with **Sign In…**, **Try Again**, **Open in Browser** and **Copy Details**.
- **Quality follows the window size:** a small window plays a lower resolution, and enlarging it switches up, to 1080p. The "…" menu shows the current quality ("Playing at 720p").
- **The music window is for music only.** A video never replaces the song, whether in a browser or a Watch player; videos keep their own windows. It changes only when the Music/Spotify song changes.
- **The same clicks as a video window:** click the middle to play/pause; double-click the left or right side to go back or forward 10 s; double-click the top or bottom middle for full screen. Space and ←/→ work too. Music and Spotify are controlled directly. macOS asks once, on your first click, so a click never pauses a video by mistake.
- **Windows don't cover each other:** a new video opens beside the music window, and the music window opens beside your videos.

## Install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

The automated build compiled the app for Apple Silicon and Intel and passed its unit tests, the bundled-tool checks, including loading the Now Playing reader, and the installer verification. Since build 51 it also plays a test video in the real player (click zones, keys, volume, resizing and the quality switch) and checks the music window's clicks and its music-only rule. It hasn't been tried by hand on a real Mac yet; that's what this preview is for. Now Playing relies on a workaround for macOS 15.4+'s restriction on reading Now Playing information. If a future macOS closes it, the Now Playing row simply doesn't appear.
