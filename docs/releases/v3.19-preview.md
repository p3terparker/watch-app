# Watch 3.19 preview — Now Playing artwork

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer preview passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18.

## New

- **Now Playing:** whatever is playing in another app (Music, Spotify, Podcasts, a browser video…) appears at the top of the panel with its artwork, title and artist. Click it to open the artwork in a floating window that follows the music. Right-click for Copy, Save, Open in Preview, Search YouTube and Show App. Watch only reads what's playing; it never pauses, skips or otherwise controls the other app, and asks for no permissions. Turn it off in Preferences ▸ "Show what's playing in other apps".
- **Full screen artwork:** double-click the artwork (or press F, or use the "…" menu) to fill the screen. Choose a background of the artwork's own colours or black under "…" ▸ Full-Screen Background. Esc leaves full screen.

## Install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

The automated build compiled the app for Apple Silicon and Intel and passed its unit tests, the bundled-tool checks, including loading the Now Playing reader, and the installer verification. It hasn't been tried by hand on a real Mac yet; that's what this preview is for. Now Playing relies on a workaround for macOS 15.4+'s restriction on reading Now Playing information. If a future macOS closes it, the Now Playing row simply doesn't appear.
