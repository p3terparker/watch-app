# Watch 3.22 preview — Podcasts

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer 3.22 build passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18; the 3.19–3.21 previews stay available.

## New: podcasts

- **Search podcasts from the field.** Search results now start with **YouTube | Podcasts** tabs. Podcasts looks up Apple's podcast directory — no account needed — and stays selected for your next searches until you switch back.
- **Show pages:** pick a show (or paste an Apple Podcasts show link or a podcast feed) to see its episodes, newest first, with date, length, a resume bar and ✓ for ones you've finished.
- **Episodes play in the music window**, with the artwork, timeline and volume — and they **resume where you left off**. Finished episodes get a ✓ (right-click to Mark as Played/Unplayed).
- **+** adds an episode to Up Next (⌥-click = Play Next), so podcasts mix with songs, files and videos in the same list. **↗** opens it in Apple Podcasts, **⬇** downloads it. Double-click the music window to open the episode in Apple Podcasts.
- **Apple Podcasts episode links** play the same way when pasted, dropped or copied (they show up in Recent Links).

## Also

- On smaller screens the Up Next list could sit on top of the music window; the window now moves up just enough for the list to fit below it.

## Install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

Built and tested automatically on GitHub's Mac: the whole app compiles. The unit checks cover Apple Podcasts links, the directory's answers, feeds and resume keys. The window checks use stand-in directory answers and a test file made by the bundled FFmpeg: searching, a show's episodes, an episode resuming at its saved spot, pausing, playing to the end (✓), and an episode link. Real searches, real feeds and real Apple Podcasts links need trying by hand. That's what this preview is for.
