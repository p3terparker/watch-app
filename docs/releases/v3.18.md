# Watch 3.18 (48) — fully bundled preview

The first standalone, open-source Watch App release. Watch lives in the macOS menu bar and plays videos in floating windows.

## Download and install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below (about 230 MB).
2. Open it and drag **Watch** to **Applications**.
3. Eject the disk image and open Watch. Follow the first-run guide, then click the menu-bar play icon or press **Option–Command–Y**.

**This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized by Apple.** If macOS blocks the first launch, try opening Watch, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. Do not disable system security. If that option is unavailable, this installer may not be usable on your Mac.

## Included

- Fully expanded, scrollable recent links and favourites in the compact panel.
- YouTube search as you type, with a short debounce; Return still works immediately.
- Floating playback, queues, resume positions, and video/audio downloads.
- A first-run guide and bundled-tool readiness checks.
- Bundled yt-dlp 2026.10.26 (including Python/EJS), FFmpeg/ffprobe 9.0.2, and Deno 2.9.8. **No Terminal, Homebrew, or separate tool installation is needed.** Existing system tools are left alone.

Public search and most public videos need no account. Optional YouTube connection uses your browser session and can require browser/Keychain or Full Disk Access permission. No account credentials or cookies are shipped.

## Requirements and verification

Requires macOS 13 or newer. The app and helpers include Apple Silicon and Intel architectures. Verification passed 499 checks covering tooling, Swift regressions, isolated AppKit behavior and real bundled helpers, plus a clean-clone check and mounted-DMG validation. Intel helpers were exercised under Rosetta; **real Intel hardware, macOS 13 and authenticated YouTube account flows remain manual acceptance gaps**. YouTube/site changes can affect playback and account features.

Watch's source is MIT licensed. Bundled components retain their own licenses; notices, pinned source archives and build instructions are inside the app under `Contents/Resources/ThirdParty`, accessible through the Setup Guide.
