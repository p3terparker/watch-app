The first release from this repository. Watch lives in the macOS menu bar and plays videos in floating windows.

## Install

1. Download **Watch.dmg** below (about 230 MB).
2. Open it and drag **Watch** to **Applications**.
3. Eject the disk image and open Watch. Follow the first-run guide, then click the menu-bar play icon or press **⌥⌘Y**.

This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS may block the first launch. Try opening Watch, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

## What's in it

- Recent links and favourites in the compact panel, fully listed and scrollable.
- YouTube search as you type, after a short pause. Return still searches right away.
- Floating playback, queues, resume positions, and video and audio downloads.
- A first-run guide that checks the bundled tools.
- yt-dlp 2026.10.26 (with Python and EJS), FFmpeg and ffprobe 9.0.2, and Deno 2.9.8 are inside the app, so there's nothing else to install.

Search and most public videos work without an account. Connecting YouTube is optional; it uses your browser's session and may need Keychain or Full Disk Access permission. No cookies or credentials are shipped with the app.

## Notes

Needs macOS 13 or later, and runs on Apple Silicon and Intel. The Intel tools were run under Rosetta only. A real Intel Mac, macOS 13 and signing in to YouTube haven't been tried. Sites and YouTube change, so playback and account features can break between releases.

Watch's source is MIT. The bundled tools keep their own licenses, and their notices and sources are inside the app (Setup Guide ▸ Open Third-Party Notices).

To update, download a newer release, quit Watch and replace it in Applications. Your settings stay.
