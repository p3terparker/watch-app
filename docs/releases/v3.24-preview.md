# Watch 3.24 preview — part of your Mac

A preview of the next Watch, built and checked automatically on GitHub's Mac. It replaces itself whenever a newer 3.24 build passes the same checks. The [latest full release](https://github.com/p3terparker/watch-app/releases/latest) is still 3.18; the 3.19–3.23 previews stay available.

## New

- **Media keys and Control Center.** Whatever Watch plays — a video window, or a song, file or podcast in the music window — now shows in macOS's Now Playing with its picture, and the keyboard's play/pause, next and previous keys, AirPods and headphone buttons control it. When only Music or Spotify is playing, the keys stay theirs.
- **Music pauses for your videos.** If Music or Spotify is playing when you start something in Watch, Watch pauses it — and plays it again when you close Watch's videos. Both are on in **Preferences ▸ General** and can be turned off. macOS asks once, the first time, for Watch to control that app.
- **Up Next is kept.** Closing the music window or quitting Watch no longer loses your list: open Up Next and choose **Resume** next to "Up Next from before".
- **Open With ▸ Watch** in Finder: a video plays in a window; audio files, folders or several files go to Up Next under the music window. Watch never makes itself the default app.

## Install

1. Download **Watch-3.24.dmg** below.
2. If Watch is running, right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Quit Watch**.
3. Open the disk image and drag **Watch** onto **Applications**, choosing **Replace**. Your links, favourites and preferences stay.
4. Open Watch. This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

## Status

Built and tested automatically on GitHub's Mac: the whole app compiles. The unit checks cover which of Watch's players owns the media keys, when Music is paused and resumed, and saving Up Next. The window checks use a stand-in Music app and test files made by the bundled FFmpeg: a video holding Now Playing and answering pause/play, Music pausing while it plays and playing again when it closes, Up Next kept after the music window closes, and an audio file opened with Open With. Real media keys, Control Center, AirPods and Finder need trying by hand. That's what this preview is for.
